Smart booking

Every new visit, placed where it fits the route.

VetSet tries the visit in every open position on every unit’s route for the next two weeks, then ranks the options by added drive time — nudged sooner when it’s urgent.

  • Honors morning or afternoon preferences and pinned times
  • Won’t push an existing client past their arrival window
  • Shows exactly where the stop lands on the map
Route optimization

Reorder the day in one tap.

Drive times come from the real road network. VetSet flags stops that will miss their window, shows the minutes and miles you’d save, and never moves pinned or in-progress visits.

  • Keep promised windows or re-plan them
  • Before-and-after drive time and miles
  • Notify each client of a new window
Doctor day sheet

What the doctor needs, stop by stop.

Each visit shows the pets with breed, age and weight, handling alerts, the reason for the visit, when they were last seen, care that’s also due, and any open balance.

  • Start and complete visits in one tap
  • Invoice created automatically on completion
  • Running-late notices for the rest of the day’s clients
Driver run sheet

Next stop, gate code, go.

A big next-stop card with the address, access notes and pet alerts, plus one-tap Google or Apple Maps navigation, a call button and an on-my-way text with the ETA.

  • Arrival tracking the office can see
  • Pre-trip vehicle checklist
  • Mileage log from odometer readings

Contact details, access notes, pets with handling alerts, upcoming visits, messages and balances — with care reminders that roll forward automatically after each visit.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is VetSet Manager for?

Mobile and house-call veterinary practices — from a single doctor with a driver to several units on the road — and the office staff who keep their days running.

Is it a medical records system?

No. VetSet handles the administrative side of a mobile practice: scheduling, routes, clients and pets, reminders, messages and billing. Medical records stay in your existing system.

How does route optimization work?

Drive times come from real road routing. VetSet tests different stop orders against each client’s promised arrival window and shows the time and miles saved before you apply anything. Pinned and in-progress visits never move.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The doctor and driver views are designed for the phone, and the office gets a full desktop layout.

Does it send texts automatically?

In the live demo, messages open in your phone’s Messages or Mail app, filled in and ready to send, and are logged to the client’s history. Automatic sending through a texting service is on the roadmap.

Where is data stored?

In the live demo, each browser keeps its own private copy of a sample practice, so you can explore freely. A shared cloud database for teams is the next step.

Is the data on this page real?

No. Every screen shows Premier Home Vet Care, a fictional sample practice with invented clients, pets, places and phone numbers.
